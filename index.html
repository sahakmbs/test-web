Siding Expert Guide

Choose Pacific Northwest-Ready Materials

Seattle's relentless rain demands moisture-resistant siding. Fiber cement and engineered wood outperform traditional vinyl in our climate. Always verify your material's moisture absorption rating before purchase.

Never Skip the Weather Barrier

A high-quality house wrap is non-negotiable in King County. We recommend breathable barriers with taped seams. This single layer prevents mold, rot, and costly interior damage.

Flashing Is Everything

Windows, doors, and corners are where 90% of siding failures begin. Proper metal flashing installation around every penetration is critical. We inspect every joint before the siding goes up.

Expansion Gaps Matter

Wood and fiber cement expand and contract with temperature swings. Leaving proper gaps at seams and corners prevents buckling and cracking. Our crews measure twice and cut once.

Paint Before You Install

Pre-painting cut edges of fiber cement or wood siding prevents water wicking into exposed cores. This small step extends siding life by years in our wet climate.

Why MasterFix?

We've completed 40+ siding projects across Seattle. Our crews understand the unique challenges of Pacific Northwest exteriors — from moisture management to wind-load requirements on hillside properties.
